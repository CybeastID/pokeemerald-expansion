## 1. Objective and scope

Establish a review and acceptance gate for competent AI across Koanko's original six, conjured six, independent allied Zacian-Crowned, and all form/phase/topology transitions. Difficulty should come from useful legal actions, coverage, recovery and sustained resource pressure. Reuse ordinary AI rather than build thirteen combatant-specific scorers or a parallel executor.

Stages 01–09 retain their feature responsibilities. Stage 11 reviews their AI integration, supplies production-profile tactical scenarios and assigns demonstrated scoring defects to the owning feature/shared helper. Stage 10 cannot claim final integration acceptance until this gate passes. This numbering preserves filenames; it does not require numerical implementation order or create a dependency on completed Stage 10.

Confirmed decisions remain authoritative: only opponent Tera-used bookkeeping resets once at team transition; Calyrex Fire Tera is optional through ordinary AI and persists through transformation only when active; Arceus displays `Alter Ego's Bug Space` while retaining Multitype. Native Stellar TX is neutral against player-accessible attack types; preserve boss-exclusive, player-unavailable Divine→Stellar 2×. Do not retune levels, kits, stats, items, bars, Tera timing or encounter scripts to make AI tests pass.

## 2. Existing code verified

### Ordinary selection and effective trainer flags

- `src/battle_ai_main.c:GetAiFlags` reads authored trainer flags, adds DOUBLE_BATTLE for an already-double battle and DYNAMIC_FUNC for a registered hook. It does not automatically enable CHECK_VIABILITY for ordinary trainers. `BattleAI_SetupFlags` assigns per-battler flags.
- `src/data/trainers.party:TRAINER_KOANKO` and generated `src/data/trainers.h` currently use CHECK_BAD_MOVE | ACE_POKEMON | OMNISCIENT | TRY_TO_FAINT. Neither CHECK_VIABILITY nor SMART_TERA is enabled. The ordinary party and corrected conjured kits/levels are present. The trainer block supplies no item inventory; held items are a separate system. Do not invent healing items for Koanko.
- `BattleAI_SetupAIData` initializes four signed scores from cached move limitations and AI_SCORE_DEFAULT=100. `ComputeBattlerDecisions` prepares prediction/switching and stores chosenMoveIndex; `BattleAI_ChooseMoveIndex` selects and reconsiders gimmicks. `ChooseMoveOrAction_Singles/Doubles` select moves/targets and randomize ties. The singles picker has **u8 temporary score storage despite s32 scores**; specialized scores outside 0–255 need a targeted comparison test before claiming ordering is safe.
- `CalcBattlerAiMovesData` caches damage/accuracy/effectiveness by attacker, defender and **real slot 0–3**. `SetAiLogicDataForTurn` rebuilds these caches for living battlers. `AI_CheckBadMove` handles duplicate Tailwind, hazards, Taunt, Encore, pending Wish, recovery at full HP, Yawn/sleep restrictions, Prankster/Dark and other prevention. Many bad actions receive a penalty such as -10, not score zero: **positive scoring alone is not proof of legality or success**.
- `AI_CheckViability` invokes existing effect/additional-effect/held-effect scoring and damaging-move comparisons. `src/battle_ai_util.c:ShouldRecover` uses actual maxHP, speed, incoming damage, Heal Block and tagged recovery RNG. `ShouldUseWishAromatherapy` considers party recovery in singles and immediate recovery in doubles. That bench-healing assumption needs qualification for trapped solo TX. Existing support logic is a foundation, not runtime proof of Koanko's final tactical quality.

### Damage, effects, switching and gimmicks

Freeze-Dry uses EFFECT_SUPER_EFFECTIVE_ON_ARG and the shared damage/type path, not a custom Koanko chart.

Searches of the three inspected AI modules found no Break Bar-aware outcome policy. Ordinary lethal estimates therefore need review before applying KO bonuses to a layer break.

## 3. Implementation design and order

### A. Baseline ordinary AI before adding custom rules

1. Add production-profile tactical fixtures first: real Koanko trainer identity/effective flags, actual registered encounter hook and intended party data. Keep injected-score fixtures for plumbing, but label them separately. Capture baseline score ordering, chosen action/target/gimmick and actual effect; do not hide a flag deficiency with test-only AI_FLAGS.
2. Recommend enabling ordinary CHECK_VIABILITY for Koanko and SMART_TERA for its eligible Calyrex decisions, retaining current OMNISCIENT/ACE behavior and first-team switching. These are proposed flag changes requiring approval, not current behavior or authorization to rewrite trainer data. Stage 01 keeps its lifecycle-only flag scope; record the approved exception before changing authored flags. Do not automatically add forced setup, risky behavior, prediction, trainer items or a separate first-six scorer. Add specialized ordinary scoring only if a concrete fixture still demonstrates a deficiency after approved flag selection.
3. Preserve present information rules: existing OMNISCIENT grants move/ability/held-item awareness, not knowledge of future RNG or permission to inspect the player's uncommitted bag/action and counterpick. Player healing scenarios test reaction to observable resulting state, not a guaranteed foresight response.

### B. Shared outcome and score parity

4. Review/factor a small shared AI outcome query that distinguishes real KO, original-layer break, saved-layer reveal, survival-floor prevention and ordinary damage. Reuse Stage 01/06/09 owner policy and existing bar storage; do not simulate a second phase/refill engine. Clip damage value to the reachable current layer and do not sum multihit overkill into covered HP. Setup/recovery/KO comparisons should not treat breaking a Charm layer as defeating the player. A layer-breaking attack can still be best; it must be valued as progress plus the known ensuing phase, not fake victory. Keep probability/range estimates separate from guarantees.
5. Reuse Stage 03's ordinary single-candidate scoring work for sampled moves and Stage 05's virtual-action valuation. Virtual Meteo needs the same damage/accuracy/type/target/effect context as normal attacks; do not send slot4/5 into four-slot caches. Wish outcomes use their exact execution eligibility, then comparable utility increments around ordinary score semantics. Compare signed scores without narrowing/truncation; test >255/negative boundary scores rather than masking the issue with arbitrary gameplay caps. Preserve ordinary-versus-virtual exact ties and the documented Smeargle tie policy.
6. Add narrow tactical adapters only for verified absent mechanics: useful TX Wish effects/cooldown and immediate Wishful Thinking benefit, layer outcomes, applicable Time Stop secondary value versus recharge, and Arceus opening protection/crushing/form-based Judgment. Existing AI already penalizes recharge; avoid double-charging it or valuing a 50% proc as certain. No adapter awards INT_MAX merely for a boss signature. In Shield Dust/Covert Cloak/immune/already-stopped cases, value only effects that the engine can actually apply.
7. Ordinary Wish on solo TX must value its own reachable position, pending Wish, immediate ability heal/growth and survival; it must not favor healing injured reserves that cannot switch into TX's trapped position. Audit all-holder versus caster-only Wishful Thinking benefit against the final approved scope; the current dispatcher visits all living holders on a successful Wish. Reciprocity values actual potential healing of living players and current TX missing HP, not revivals/reserve healing or theoretical full heals. Reduction values long-term persistent pressure, but cannot reset/stack or override a demonstrably better immediate winning action. Outcome scoring remains owned by 04–05.

## 5. Edge cases and invariants

- First-team switching is ordinary; conjured order/no living switching and normal faint replacement remain Stage01 policy. Sequence flags cannot leak backward into the original team or player/ally command ownership.
- Score evaluation never changes real HP/PP, wish availability, cooldown, bar layers, inventory, ability state, Tera consumption, owner history or action cursor. Test simulation save/restore even for early return and changing dynamic types. Score zero and “cannot execute” are different facts; filter using the authoritative legality state as well as score.
- Available player Full Restore/Max Revive/Ether and one-use Break Charm are legitimate inputs. React to their actual results on fresh choices; no new forced bag prediction, retaliation turn or player item prohibition. Healing/restoring PP must invalidate relevant estimates normally. TX Reciprocity ignores reserve healing/revival and bars do not manufacture extra healing credit.
- A predicted layer break is not a genuine KO; multi-hit overkill cannot consume the next layer. Recovery targets current actual maxHP, not total layer HP. Known phase consequences may influence scoring without executing their scripts during simulation.
- Setup has no value at a capped relevant stage, when its only attacks cannot exploit it, or when known crushing immediately removes its benefit. Avoid infinite setup/recovery/Protect loops in controlled fixtures, while allowing legitimate sustain in a real endurance fight.
- Changes after selection retain ordinary timing. Encore/Disable/Taunt/Choice/Imprison/Heal Block, newly fainted targets, delayed slots and cooldown are revalidated. Failed casts spend no invented success effect; no emergency virtual substitution/second turn.
- No Tera reset beyond the one confirmed opponent transition reset; active Fire Tera remains active across Calyrex change and inactive stays optional. Mega visuals on Eternamax do not give it gimmick mechanics. Zacian never reads/consumes the player's gimmick state or inventory.
- Preserve the exact Bug Space presentation/real Multitype separation, actual converted Judgment type and player-inaccessible Divine rule. Do not make test-only move access legitimate gameplay.

### Tactical fixtures and assertions

New cases use `Koanko AI` plus combatant/category suffixes (`Original`, `Smeargle`, `TX`, `Calyrex`, `Eternatus`, `Dialga`, `Arceus`, `Zacian`, `Cache`, `Commit`, `Topology`, `Resources`). For each matrix row, record: actual trainer/effective flags; legal action set; score ordering or allowed tie set; chosen move/outcome/target/gimmick; controller commitment; executed HP/status/PP/bar/phase result. Use SCORE_GT for a controlled strict improvement, SCORE_EQ/EXPECT_MOVES for defensible alternatives, and exact EXPECT_MOVE only when a fixture makes one action unambiguously better. Do not assert one scripted move on every turn. Test probability-dependent choices with tagged RNG/known seed sets and document the allowed rate, not a demand for universal selection.

Each combatant needs at least one productive-choice case and one failed/redundant-choice avoidance case, plus the specialized cases in its row. Provide boundary fixtures for max/full HP, missing HP, pending Wish/delayed slots, existing status/hazards, capped stages, immunities and relevant speed/priority.
